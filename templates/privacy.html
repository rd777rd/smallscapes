Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

SmallScapes LLC ("we", "us") operates this website to share information about our hardscaping and landscaping services. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit a review through our Reviews page, we collect the name, star rating, and comment you provide. We do not require or collect your email address, phone number, or physical address through the review form.

How We Use Information

Submitted reviews are held for staff approval before publishing on our public Reviews page. We use the information solely to display testimonials from our clients and to respond to inquiries if you contact us directly by phone or email.

Contacting Us

If you call or email us directly using the contact details on this site, that communication is handled the same way any small business would handle a phone call or email — we do not sell or share your contact information with third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

This site does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts.
